About PakStudentTools

Empowering Pakistani students with instant, accurate, and completely free academic admission tools.

Why PakStudentTools Exists

Every admission season in Pakistan, hundreds of thousands of students navigate college and university admissions across intermediate boards, medical entrance tests, and engineering universities. Calculating admission merit—whether it's the 10/40/50 PM&DC medical formula, UET's ECAT split, or university-specific criteria like UAF—is often frustrating. Formulas are typically buried in multi-page prospectus PDFs, scattered across conflicting social media posts, or hosted on slow, ad-choked websites that require unnecessary signups.

PakStudentTools was created to solve this problem by providing clean, fast, instant, and mobile-friendly calculators that compute accurate merit percentages according to official government and university criteria.

A Student-Built Project

PakStudentTools is an independent, student-built initiative. We developed this platform because we experienced the exact same stress and confusion during our own Matric, FSc, and entrance test journeys. We wanted to build the kind of tool we wished had existed when we were applying: completely free, transparent about its mathematical formulas, without paywalls, and built to run smoothly even on slower 3G/4G mobile internet connections across Pakistan.

Our Core Principles

  • 100% Free & Open: No accounts, no subscriptions, and no paywalls. All calculation tools are freely accessible to every student.
  • Client-Side Privacy: Your academic marks belong to you. All calculations run locally in your web browser—no personal scores or entered numbers are ever transmitted to or stored on our servers.
  • Official & Verified Formulas: Every tool uses published criteria directly verified against official bodies such as PM&DC, UET, provincial boards (BISE), and university prospectuses.
  • Mobile-First & Accessible: Designed from the ground up to work seamlessly on smartphones, with lightweight code that loads in under a second.

Support & Community Feedback

Because admission policies and test formats can evolve from year to year, we actively maintain and update our formulas. If you notice a recent policy change by an admission authority or want to suggest a new university calculator, please visit our Contact page to reach out.
