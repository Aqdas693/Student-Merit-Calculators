Notice a Formula Discrepancy or Policy Change?

We strive for 100% mathematical and regulatory accuracy. Pakistani educational boards, PM&DC, and university syndicates occasionally revise admission weighting ratios (for example, adjusting entrance test percentages or changing test total marks).

If any formula on this site differs from your institution's latest official prospectus, please send us:

  • The name of the calculator tool.
  • The exact formula or weightage change.
  • A link to the official university circular, gazette, or prospectus page verifying the update.

We review and update criteria promptly so other students can benefit from accurate information.
