Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

At PakStudentTools, accessible from student-merit-calculators.vercel.app, we believe in complete transparency and straightforward, plain-language privacy practices. This document explains what information is processed when you visit our website, how cookies are used, and your rights as a visitor.

1. Academic Calculator Inputs (Zero Server Data Collection)

When you use any calculator on our website (including the Matric/FSc Aggregate, MDCAT Merit, ECAT Merit, or UAF Merit calculators), none of the marks, test scores, or numbers you enter are sent to our servers.

All calculations are performed entirely client-side using JavaScript within your own web browser. Once you close or reload the browser tab, any numbers you entered are immediately discarded. We do not store, collect, log, profile, or sell your academic marks or calculated merit results.

2. Google Analytics (Site Usage & Performance)

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our website (such as which calculators are most popular, common device screen sizes, and general geographic country/city data).

Google Analytics collects non-personally identifiable information via browser cookies. This includes:

  • Browser type, operating system, and screen resolution (to help us optimize mobile layouts).
  • Pages viewed, visit duration, and referring websites.
  • Anonymized IP addresses (we do not see your individual IP address or physical location).

You can prevent Google Analytics from tracking your visits by disabling cookies in your browser settings or installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

To keep all calculator tools 100% free for students, PakStudentTools is monetized through advertising provided by Google AdSense and affiliated third-party advertising networks.

Please note the following regarding how advertising vendors operate on this site:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites across the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.
  • For detailed information on how Google manages data in its advertising products, please review Google's Privacy & Terms on Advertising.

4. External Links

Our website contains links to official university portals (such as ecat.uet.edu.pk), board portals, and educational regulatory bodies. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party websites.

5. Updates to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our tools or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to us via our Contact page.
