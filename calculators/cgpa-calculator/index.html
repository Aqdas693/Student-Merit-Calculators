Understanding the Semester CGPA Calculation in Pakistani Universities

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) represents the overall academic performance of a university student across multiple semesters. In Pakistani higher education institutions governed by the Higher Education Commission (HEC), students receive a Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) at the end of each term, reflecting their performance in courses completed during that specific session. CGPA, by contrast, is the cumulative credit-hour-weighted average of all semesters completed to date.

CGPA = ∑(Semester GPA × Semester Credit Hours) ÷ Total Credit Hours

A simple unweighted average of your semester GPAs is inaccurate because academic semesters frequently carry unequal course loads. HEC grading guidelines mandate that each semester's contribution must be weighted proportionally according to its total credit hours. Multiplying each semester's GPA by its credit hours yields the Quality Points (QP) earned in that term. Dividing total accumulated Quality Points by total completed credit hours produces your true composite CGPA.

While this credit-weighted method is universally standardized across Pakistani universities—including NUST, FAST-NUCES, UET, COMSATS, GIKI, and Punjab University—specific institutional transcript policies can influence final figures. If you have repeated courses, availed grade replacement or academic forgiveness policies, or transferred credits from another institution, your official university registrar's calculations may vary. Always cross-check with your official student portal or controller of examinations.
