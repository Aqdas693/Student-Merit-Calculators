Understanding the Matric & FSc Aggregate Formula

In Pakistan, admission to competitive undergraduate degree programs—including BS Engineering, Computer Science, Pre-Medical, and Social Sciences—relies on academic merit aggregates. Rather than evaluating raw totals alone, universities and provincial admission authorities calculate a weighted aggregate combining your secondary (Matric/SSC) and higher secondary (FSc/HSSC Part 1 and Part 2) examination results.

Aggregate (%) = (Matric Obt ÷ Total × 10) + (FSc-I Obt ÷ Total × 40) + (FSc-II Obt ÷ Total × 50)

The standard calculation formula determines your percentage score in each academic tier and applies official weightages. By default, Matriculation contributes 10%, FSc Part 1 contributes 40%, and FSc Part 2 contributes 50% toward the final composite score. This weighted structure places the greatest emphasis on your recent intermediate academic rigor while acknowledging foundational board results.

Students can compare their calculated aggregate directly against previous years' closing merit lists for institutions such as Punjab University (PU), GCU Lahore, and affiliated public degree colleges. While most public universities adhere to this 10:40:50 distribution, some specialized programs may apply alternative weightings, which can easily be configured in the calculator settings.
