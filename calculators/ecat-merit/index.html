Understanding the ECAT & Engineering Merit Formula

The ECAT Merit Calculator helps prospective engineering students determine their admission aggregate for public engineering universities across Punjab, primarily the University of Engineering and Technology (UET) Lahore and its affiliated campuses. Under the standard UET admission criteria, merit is calculated using a composite weighted score combining Matriculation, FSc Pre-Engineering, and the official Engineering College Admission Test (ECAT):

Aggregate (%) = (Matric Obt ÷ Total × 17) + (FSc Obt ÷ Total × 50) + (ECAT Obt ÷ 400 × 33)

Because the ECAT exam is evaluated out of a maximum of 400 marks, your test percentage is calculated directly against 400 and scaled to 33% of your final aggregate.

Important Note on University Variations: It is crucial to recognize that there is no universal merit formula across Pakistani engineering institutions. While UET Lahore and Punjab public colleges utilize this ECAT-based formula, other leading engineering universities follow distinct criteria. For example, UET Peshawar conducts its own entrance test through the Educational Testing and Evaluation Agency (ETEA) with different weightings, while the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST) conducts the NET test and weights it at 75% alongside 15% FSc and 10% Matric. Always verify specific departmental criteria against your target university's official admission prospectus.
