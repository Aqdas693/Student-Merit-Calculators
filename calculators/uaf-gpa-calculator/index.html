Understanding the UAF Quality Points & GPA Calculation System

The University of Agriculture Faisalabad (UAF) utilizes a unique, standardized Quality Points system to evaluate undergraduate and postgraduate academic performance. Unlike institutions that compute Grade Point Average (GPA) through a straight percentage formula or simple letter grades, UAF assigns Quality Points directly based on a student's raw obtained marks and the credit hour weight of each individual course.

Semester GPA = (Total Quality Points Earned) ÷ (Total Course Credit Hours)

Every course at UAF is evaluated out of a maximum mark proportional to its credit hours (20 marks per credit hour: 1 Cr = 20, 2 Cr = 40, 3 Cr = 60, 4 Cr = 80, and 5 Cr = 100). The official UAF Quality Point Schedule maps obtained marks within each tier to discrete Quality Points. Marks falling below the minimum passing threshold (e.g., under 40% of maximum marks) yield 0.00 Quality Points (F grade). This calculator incorporates the exact official UAF lookup table rather than a mathematical approximation, ensuring that your computed semester GPA and cumulative CGPA match your official university transcript.

Looking for Admission Merit? Please note that this calculator computes semester and cumulative GPA for enrolled university students.
