Understanding the UAF Undergraduate Merit Formula

The UAF Merit Calculator computes your official admission aggregate for undergraduate degree programs at the University of Agriculture Faisalabad (UAF). This confirmed calculation formula applies across UAF's main campus in Faisalabad as well as its sub-campuses in Toba Tek Singh, Burewala (Vehari), and Depalpur (Okara).

Aggregate (%) = (Matric Obt ÷ Total × 30) + (Inter Part-I Obt ÷ Total × 30) + (UAF Test Obt ÷ Total × 40)

Undergraduate admissions at UAF—including competitive programs such as Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM), BSc (Hons) Agriculture, BS Computer Science, Agricultural Engineering, and Food Science & Technology—are evaluated under UAF's official 30:30:40 weighting scheme.

UAF administers its own centralized entry test in multiple test streams each admissions season. Because undergraduate admissions commence before 12th-grade board results are declared, UAF officially evaluates Intermediate achievement using First Year (Part-I) marks.

This calculator incorporates the confirmed official UAF weighting criteria, replacing the earlier placeholder version. Students can use their computed aggregate score to evaluate their admission standing against previous closing merit lists across all UAF faculties and campuses.
