Understanding the PM&DC MDCAT Merit Formula

The MDCAT Merit Calculator is designed for aspiring medical and dental students seeking admission to public and private sector MBBS and BDS programs across Pakistan. Admissions are governed by the Pakistan Medical and Dental Council (PM&DC) and executed provincially by examining authorities including the University of Health Sciences (UHS) in Punjab, Dow University of Health Sciences (DUHS) in Sindh, Khyber Medical University (KMU) in KPK, Bolan University of Medical & Health Sciences (BUMHS) in Balochistan, and SZABMU in Islamabad.

Aggregate (%) = (Matric Obt ÷ Total × 10) + (FSc Obt ÷ Total × 40) + (MDCAT Obt ÷ 180 × 50)

The official PM&DC merit formula combines your academic history and entrance test achievement into a single composite percentage. Because the standard national MDCAT examination comprises 180 total marks, your score is calculated directly against this 180-mark base and weighted to represent 50% of your total admission aggregate.

Note on NUMS Admissions: The National University of Medical Sciences (NUMS) also follows the identical 10% Matric, 40% FSc, and 50% entry test formula for Army Medical College (AMC) and affiliated military institutions. However, the NUMS entry test is scored out of 200 marks rather than 180. Consequently, NUMS merit scores must be computed separately using their 200-mark denominator and are not directly interchangeable with provincial PM&DC MDCAT scores.
