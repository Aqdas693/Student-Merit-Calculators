Understanding the NUMS Medical Admission Merit Formula

The NUMS Merit Calculator helps prospective medical and dental students determine their admission aggregate for the National University of Medical Sciences (NUMS) in Rawalpindi. NUMS administers admissions for its premier constituent institution, Army Medical College (AMC), as well as numerous affiliated private medical and dental colleges across Pakistan.

Aggregate (%) = (Matric Obt ÷ Total × 10) + (FSc Obt ÷ Total × 40) + (NUMS Obt ÷ 200 × 50)

Unlike provincial medical admissions that rely on the central PM&DC MDCAT, NUMS conducts its own dedicated entrance test with distinct syllabus specifications. The standard NUMS Entry Test comprises 200 total marks (consisting of subject-based multiple-choice questions and a psychological assessment component), evaluated separately from the national 180-mark MDCAT.

This calculator applies the widely cited simplified admission formula: 10% Matriculation, 40% Intermediate (FSc Pre-Medical), and 50% NUMS Entry Test. Please note that while this baseline formula is standard for general estimations, NUMS may implement tiered or category-specific weightings—distinguishing open-merit seats at Army Medical College from paying or private affiliated colleges, or adjusting foreign and quota requirements. Candidates should always verify candidate seat categories and specific eligibility criteria in the official NUMS prospectus.
